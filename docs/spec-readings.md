# Spec readings

Places where SPEC.md leaves room for more than one reading, the reading the
code takes, and why. Each was checked against the spec text. One reading
contradicted the spec and was changed (marked below); the rest stand.

| Section | Reading | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| §5.1 odds gate | "The known enemy mobile ground army metal" leaves out enemy commanders. | Our side of the ratio counts combat units only (the spec leaves our commander out). Counting theirs would make the two sides unequal, and a commander's metal (2500 in the sim) would keep the gate shut for most of the early game. |
| §5.7 commander fight or flee | "The threat metal" is the metal of the commander's danger contacts: seen, standing, armed, non-air, non-frame contacts within `cmd.danger_radius` (or the weapon range of a contact on water). | The fight test belongs to the commander-danger rule, and those contacts are what the commander then fights, d-guns or flees from. The exposure sum earlier in §5.7 also counts contacts not seen for up to 150 ticks and long-range units well outside 450, which would make the commander flee from threats that aren't there. |
| §5.3 counter lean | A role triggers when its count's share of the *whole* histogram is at least `mix.counter_trigger`. | This is what the text says ("its share of the histogram"). The histogram includes static defence, which is one of the roles that triggers a lean, so leaving buildings out of the total would not make sense. |
| §5.7 chase leash | The 675 leash (1.5 × `cmd.danger_radius`) is measured from the commander to its target. | It is a radius round the commander, like the danger radius it is scaled from. The spec gives no other anchor, such as a start point or the frame. |
| §4.3 rule 0, test only | The economy test lets energy stay stalled for at most 30 s in a row. | The spec sets no tolerance. Rule 0 makes a solar the next job, but the builder may be one job (and a walk) away, and the solar takes time to build. 30 s covers that reaction and still catches a missing answer. It is a test bound, not an AI behaviour. |
| §5.8 air patrol | The "base edge" is the distance from home to the furthest own building. | The spec uses the term without defining it. §4.6 uses "outermost own building's distance" for the same idea, and the clamp to 200..900 covers tiny and sprawling bases. |
| §5.9 ferry landing stall | A stalled loaded sea hull puts its landing point's `ferry.refusal_cell` cell in the refusal memory for `ferry.refusal_s`, then searches again from where it is. | This follows the spec text. The refusal memory is the same one the objective search reads (§5.2), so the army also avoids that landing. |
| §5.12, §5.5 navy "same water" (changed) | "Same water" is the station's water body, the body of the fleet's station. Objectives, submarine hunts and the commander guard use it. Targets must also pass the engine's can-hit test. | The commander guard used to check only distance and can-hit. This contradicted "on the same water": a hull in a pond behind the commander was sent after a ship it could not reach. Now only hulls on the station's body guard (Fix: Guard the commander only from our water). |
| §4.2 saving (new) | Saving starts whether or not a site exists. It ends when the saved-for item is affordable but has no site. | The spec says saving does not depend on a site, and that only economy types may be taken "until the saved-for item is built". If the item has no site it is never built, so the saving would never end. A land mirror showed this: metal full, every builder idle for ten minutes. Ending the saving only once the item is affordable keeps "saving does not depend on a site" for the whole saving window. |
